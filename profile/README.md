# AI Imaging Lab

We study fundamental methods in computational imaging, computer vision, and machine learning. Our research aims to advance the way we capture, analyze, and reconstruct the world around us, bridging theoretical advances and practical applications across a wide range of fields, from computational photography to creative AI.

## Repositories

This organization hosts official implementations, research code, and supporting resources from the AI Imaging Lab. Please refer to each repository for project-specific documentation, licenses, citation information, and issue tracking.

> For current news, the complete publication list, member information, and research opportunities, please visit our [official website](https://aii.korea.ac.kr), which serves as the canonical source.
